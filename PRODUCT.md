# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Tech founders, VP of Engineering, Heads of Product, and Enterprise Decision-Makers seeking high-end digital products, AI systems, automation infrastructure, and custom software architecture.
Secondary: Design directors, agency partners, and prospective elite technical talent looking to collaborate with an Awwwards-caliber digital studio.

## Product Purpose

Establish REKASANDI as an elite digital studio whose public web platform serves as direct living proof of the craft, speed, and architectural sophistication it delivers for clients. The primary business success metric is generating qualified inbound inquiries through high-intent "Start a Project" pathways.

## Positioning

Indonesia-rooted, internationally-capable digital product architecture studio. Positioned strictly as a high-craft engineering & design partner rather than a commodity IT vendor or dev-shop. Competes with top-tier global studios on architectural depth, sub-second performance, tactile editorial aesthetics, and enterprise reliability.

## Operating Context

- **Client Evaluation Rituals**: High-stakes procurement and technical due-diligence where prospective clients evaluate case studies, live previews, performance metrics, and technological depth before initiating contact.
- **Inquiry & Project Intake**: Direct multi-channel initiation (project inquiry modal, structured scope forms, direct email/WhatsApp channel, and scheduled advisory calls).
- **Editorial Management**: Headless CMS administration via Payload CMS 3.x for publishing case studies, architectural whitepapers, and engineering insights.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities**:
  - Full-stack digital product engineering (Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript).
  - Autonomous AI workflows & multi-agent reasoning systems (Python, LangChain, PostgreSQL/pgvector).
  - High-density telemetry & mission-control dashboards (WebGL, Mapbox, WebSockets).
  - Headless CMS with real-time live preview and automated type generation (Payload CMS 3.x, SQLite/PostgreSQL dual-adapter).
  - SEO-native metadata engine with dynamic sitemaps and structured JSON-LD schemas.
- **Constraints**:
  - Light-mode editorial paper aesthetic (#f7f6f2) with dark ink typography (#0a0a0a) and subtle electric lime (#d7ff3f) accents.
  - Zero heavy unoptimized dependencies; 60fps GPU-accelerated motion (Lenis + Framer Motion) with mandatory `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
  - Strict nominal TypeScript types with zero `any`.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: REKASANDI Digital Studio ("Rekasandi").

## Product Principles

1. **The Product is the Proof**: The platform itself must embody the technical excellence, motion restraint, and typography Rekasandi sells to clients.
2. **Editorial Depth over Surface Polish**: Prioritize substantive technical case studies, concrete operational metrics, and transparent architectural reasoning over superficial flashiness.
3. **Restrained Power**: High-density engineering authority delivered with calm typography, generous negative space, and disciplined micro-interactions.
4. **Instantaneous Feedback**: Zero layout shifts, hardware-accelerated transitions, and sub-second page loads that feel instantaneous on any device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA standards across all interactive elements.
- Strict `prefers-reduced-motion` handling for all line-mask reveals, parallax layers, and motion graphics.
- Keyboard-navigable dialogs, lightboxes, and project filters with visible high-contrast focus rings.
