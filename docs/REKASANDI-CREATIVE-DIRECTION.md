# REKASANDI — Creative Direction & Design System Brief

> **Premium Digital Studio / Awwwards-Level Digital Experience**

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Design Direction  
**Brand:** Rekasandi  
**Product:** Premium Digital Studio Website  
**Primary Stack:** Next.js 16.x · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · shadcn/ui · Payload CMS · PostgreSQL  
**Motion Stack:** Framer Motion · GSAP · Lenis

---

## 1. Creative Objective

The Rekasandi website should not look like a conventional Indonesian software-house website.

It should feel like a **premium digital product studio** whose website itself demonstrates the quality of its work.

The experience should communicate:

- Craft
- Confidence
- Intelligence
- Precision
- Technology
- Creativity
- Trust
- Restraint

The design must feel contemporary without depending on temporary visual trends.

### Core Principle

> **The website is Rekasandi's first product.**

If Rekasandi sells digital products, the website must demonstrate product thinking, visual craft, interaction design, and engineering quality before a visitor ever contacts the company.

---

# 2. Brand Positioning

## Desired Perception

A visitor should perceive Rekasandi as:

**Premium** — high attention to detail and execution.

**Technical** — capable of solving difficult software problems.

**Creative** — able to create distinctive digital experiences.

**Strategic** — understands business objectives, not only implementation.

**Reliable** — professional enough for serious business engagements.

**Modern** — comfortable with AI and emerging technologies.

---

# 3. Brand Personality

The visual language should balance opposing qualities.

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| Premium | Quiet, refined, intentional |
| Technical | Precise, structured, systematic |
| Creative | Unexpected compositions |
| Professional | Clear and trustworthy |
| Experimental | Controlled, never chaotic |
| Minimal | Strong hierarchy rather than emptiness |
| Bold | Typography and composition rather than excessive effects |

### Avoid

- Generic SaaS gradients
- Excessive purple/blue AI aesthetics
- Stock photos of people coding
- Generic laptop mockups everywhere
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Neon effects without purpose
- Template-like card grids
- Overly corporate copy
- Animation on every element
- Decorative 3D objects with no narrative purpose

---

# 4. Creative Concept

## Primary Concept: "Engineered Clarity"

Rekasandi takes complex ideas and turns them into clear digital experiences.

This concept should influence the entire visual system.

Complexity can be represented through:

- Layers
- Grids
- Data-like structures
- Modular typography
- Dense information

Clarity is represented through:

- Strong hierarchy
- Large whitespace
- Clear typography
- Precise alignment
- Controlled motion

The visual story becomes:

```text
COMPLEXITY
     ↓
STRUCTURE
     ↓
DESIGN
     ↓
ENGINEERING
     ↓
CLARITY
```

## Overall Feel

The art direction should be:

> **Editorial + Technology + Digital Craft**

Think less "software company" and more:

- Independent digital studio
- Design engineering practice
- Editorial technology publication
- Contemporary architecture studio
- High-end product design consultancy

# 6. Visual Language

The design should use a small number of strong visual ingredients.

### Primary Ingredients

1. Oversized typography
2. Editorial whitespace
3. Strict grid
4. High-quality project imagery
5. Asymmetric composition
6. Subtle motion
7. Strong contrast
8. Micro-interactions
9. Controlled transitions
10. Technical details

The goal is to make relatively simple components feel sophisticated through composition.

- Large vertical rhythm
- Maximum content width
- Intentional asymmetry

# 10. Typography Direction

Typography is one of the primary brand assets.

The website should use a strong sans-serif system with excellent:

- Large display rendering
- Tight tracking
- Variable weights
- Numerals
- Responsive scaling

## Recommended Candidates

### Option A — Geist

Modern, technical, neutral.

### Option B — Instrument Sans

More editorial and expressive.

### Option C — Inter Tight

Highly functional with strong display proportions.

### Option D — Neue Montreal

More distinctive and studio-oriented.

### Option E — General Sans

Contemporary and versatile.

# 16. Hero Motion

The hero should not simply fade in.

Recommended sequence:

```text
Page load
    ↓
Logo / navigation appears
    ↓
Headline clips upward
    ↓
Secondary copy follows
    ↓
CTA appears
    ↓
Background detail subtly moves
```

Animation duration:

```text
Primary:
700–1000ms

Secondary:
400–700ms

Easing:
Cubic / custom ease-out
```

Avoid excessive bounce.

# 30. Magnetic Interaction

Apply only to major CTAs.

Recommended strength:

```text
10–20px maximum displacement
```

The interaction should feel physical, not exaggerated.

---

# 31. Smooth Scrolling

Use Lenis as the smooth-scroll layer.

Requirements:

- Maintain native-feeling scrolling
- Respect reduced motion
- Avoid excessive inertia
- Keep touch scrolling natural

Smooth scrolling should enhance the experience rather than become noticeable technology.

---

# 32. Motion Architecture

Use different tools for different responsibilities.

## Framer Motion

Use for:

- Component transitions
- Reveals
- Hover states
- Menu transitions
- Layout animations

## GSAP

Use for:

- ScrollTrigger
- Pinned sections
- Complex timelines
- Horizontal sections
- Advanced project transitions

## Lenis

Use for:

- Smooth scrolling

## CSS

Use for:

- Simple hover
- Transform
- Opacity
- Basic transitions

Prefer CSS when JavaScript is unnecessary.

---

# 33. Motion Timing

Recommended baseline:

| Interaction | Duration |
|---|---:|
| Micro interaction | 150–250ms |
| Button hover | 200–350ms |
| Standard reveal | 500–800ms |
| Hero transition | 700–1200ms |
| Page transition | 600–1000ms |
| Complex sequence | 1000–1800ms |

Motion should generally use ease-out curves.

---

# 34. Reduced Motion

When:

```text
prefers-reduced-motion: reduce
```

The system should:

- Disable smooth scrolling
- Reduce parallax
- Remove cursor effects
- Reduce transition duration
- Disable decorative movement
- Preserve functional transitions where possible

Accessibility takes priority over visual effects.

Keep radius restrained.


# 39. Texture and Detail

Optional subtle details:

- Fine grid lines
- Technical labels
- Coordinates
- Project numbers
- Small monospace metadata
- Fine borders
- Grain/noise

Use them sparingly.

---

# 40. 3D / WebGL Policy

Three.js / React Three Fiber is **optional**.

Use it only when the concept benefits from:

- Product visualization
- Generative graphics
- Interactive objects
- Brand experiments
- Data visualization
- Spatial interfaces

Do not use 3D merely to imitate other Awwwards websites.

The default Rekasandi experience should remain excellent without WebGL.

---

# 41. Video Direction

Use video only when it adds narrative value.

Good uses:

- Product demo
- Project showcase
- Process footage
- Abstract visual identity
- Cinematic background moment

Avoid autoplaying large videos everywhere.

Requirements:

- Poster image
- Lazy loading
- Optimized encoding
- Mobile fallback
- Reduced-motion consideration

# 47. Accessibility Design

Accessibility must be built into the design system.

Requirements:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Accessible forms
- Alt text
- Correct heading hierarchy
- Color contrast
- Screen-reader-friendly navigation
- Reduced motion support

Visual sophistication must never compromise usability.

---

# 48. Performance Design Principles

The visual system must be designed with performance in mind.

## Images

Use responsive image sizing and modern formats.

## Fonts

Limit font families and weights.

## Animation

Prefer GPU-friendly transforms:

```text
transform
opacity
```

Avoid unnecessary layout-triggering animation.

## JavaScript

Do not turn every visual effect into a client component.

Keep server-rendered content server-side whenever possible.

- Cursor experiments

Keep:

---

# 56. Design Quality Checklist

Before approving a section, ask:

### Visual

- Does it look distinctive?
- Is the hierarchy obvious?
- Is there enough whitespace?
- Is the typography intentional?

### Interaction

- Does motion communicate something?
- Is hover behavior useful?
- Does the interaction feel natural?

### Brand

- Does this feel like Rekasandi?
- Could this be mistaken for a generic SaaS template?

### Performance

- Does this require unnecessary JavaScript?
- Is media optimized?
- Does animation affect responsiveness?

### Accessibility

- Can the section be used without a mouse?
- Does reduced motion work?
- Is contrast sufficient?

---

# 57. Awwwards-Level Quality Bar

The goal is not to copy an Awwwards-winning website.

The goal is to reach the same level of:

- Art direction
- Detail
- Originality
- Motion quality
- Technical execution
- Content presentation
- Performance

### Success Definition

A visitor should be able to look at the Rekasandi website and say:

> **"This looks like a company that can build this level of digital product."**

That is the ultimate design objective.

# 60. Final Creative Direction

Rekasandi should occupy a space between:

```text
DESIGN STUDIO
       +
TECHNOLOGY COMPANY
       +
PRODUCT ENGINEERING STUDIO
```

The visual identity should communicate:

```text
PRECISION
    +
CRAFT
    +
TECHNOLOGY
    +
CLARITY
```

The website should be **bold in typography, restrained in color, sophisticated in motion, editorial in composition, and obsessive about detail**.

Most importantly:

> **Do not design Rekasandi to look like an IT company. Design it to look like the studio an ambitious company would hire to build its next digital product.**
